About Us

Who We Are

Vedant Global Impex is a professional export-import, transportation, clearing and forwarding company focused on providing reliable logistics support to businesses across India.

We work with dedication, transparency and responsibility to ensure that every shipment is handled safely, efficiently and on time.

As a group company operating under Megha Transport Service, we bring practical transport experience, industry knowledge and a strong service approach to every client requirement.

Our Mission

To provide dependable export-import and logistics solutions with focus on cargo safety, timely delivery and customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To become a trusted logistics and global trade partner for companies looking for smooth, secure and professional movement of cargo.
